FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Who is this for?

Individual MFDs, distributor firms, sub-distributors and financial planning firms who have, or want, a website.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. We review your current site against SEBI and AMFI expectations, then redesign it with current disclosures and faster pages, keeping your content and brand.

Is this legal advice?

No. Aina builds and writes websites; regulations change, and final approval of content rests with you and your compliance advisor.

Do you also help with SEO?

Yes. We set up local SEO and your Google Business Profile, and suggest helpful investor content.
